## FAQ

**Q: Is the open-source version feature-complete?**

Yes. The Apache-2.0 version includes all core features: policy enforcement, capability tokens, approvals, tamper-evident logs, operator UI, and the 47ers library. Commercial engagements provide expert deployment and support, not locked features.

**Q: What's included in the pilot fee?**

Everything listed in the Pilot section above — fixed price, no hidden costs. Additional agents or workflows beyond the agreed scope may require a change order.

**Q: Do we own the deployment after a pilot?**

Yes. Pilots deploy UAPK Gateway to **your** infrastructure. You own the deployment. After the pilot, you can self-manage (open-source) or move to Enterprise Support.

**Q: What if the pilot doesn't meet objectives?**

We work closely with you to define success criteria upfront. If agreed objectives aren't met, there's no obligation to continue. Our goal is your production deployment, not billable hours.

**Q: Can we start with the Blueprint and then move to a Pilot?**

Yes — that's the recommended path when you need buy-in before committing to a full implementation. **Blueprint fee is credited in full toward a Pilot if you engage within 90 days.**

**Q: Do you offer volume discounts for multiple workflows?**

Yes. Contact us if you have multiple workflows or business units — we can structure an engagement that covers them efficiently.

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**

Bank transfer, SEPA, or credit card (invoiced). Annual contracts available at a discount. We can accommodate most procurement processes including PO-based billing.
